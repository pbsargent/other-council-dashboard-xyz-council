Start here

Quick start

  1. Confirm the refresh date in the left sidebar. This dashboard is a snapshot, not a live connection.
  2. Start on Overview for the council-wide picture, then choose a district when you need a narrower view.
  3. Use the detail pages for membership, training, unit metrics, SYT, or commissioner follow-up.
  4. Use search and sort controls to find the records that need attention. Clear the controls to return to the full view.
  5. Verify consequential decisions against the source workbook and the council systems of record.

Navigation

What each page shows

Overview
Council KPIs, district comparisons, signals, priority units, and training highlights, and coverage.
Training
Training completion, direct-contact readiness, honor rolls, missing BALOO coverage, and untrained position records when available.
Districts
Membership movement, retention, unit health, training, SYT, leadership contacts, and priority signals by district.
Unit Metrics
Unit-health score distributions and the units scoring 0–2 that may warrant follow-up.
SYT Detail
Safeguarding Youth Training status, expiration windows, and exception records from the visible workbook tab.
Coverage
Commissioner roster, training and YPT fields, assigned units, duplicate roles, and district rollups.

Controls

Filters, search, and tables

  • The District control changes the page to one district. “Council” restores the council-wide view.
  • Page-specific controls may filter by status, section, training state, expiration window, or another relevant field.
  • Search is not case-sensitive and checks the visible subject fields described in its placeholder.
  • Wide tables scroll horizontally. Table headers remain visible while the table scrolls vertically.
  • Counts and charts update with the active filters. Filtering changes the view, not the source data.

Reference

Metric definitions

Youth
Current youth membership reported in the source workbook.
YoY
Year-over-year change from the comparison membership value. Positive is growth; negative is decline.
Units
Units represented in the applicable workbook total or filtered scope.
Unit Metric
A workbook-provided unit-health score. Scores 0–2 are shown as priority follow-up; 4–5 are counted as healthy.
At Risk
The count or share of units in the priority 0–2 metric range.
Retention
The membership-retention value reported by the workbook; it is not inferred from the dashboard’s current filters.
Training
The share of applicable position records marked trained in the available workbook training data.
Direct Contact
Training results limited to positions classified as direct-contact leadership in the source.
PIN
The PIN measure supplied on the workbook Membership tab.
SYT Detail
Safeguarding Youth Training detail can include current, due-soon, and expired records when supplied on a visible source tab.
Coverage
Commissioner people and assignments found on visible source tabs.
n/a
The value was missing, unavailable, or could not be calculated. It should not be read as zero.

Interpretation

Status and color

On Track indicates the strongest current combination of available measures.

Monitor indicates a mixed signal worth watching.

Needs Attention indicates that one or more priority thresholds were triggered.

Colors and status labels are review cues based on dashboard thresholds. They are not official performance determinations and should not be used alone for personnel or program decisions.

Responsible use

Privacy and sharing

Some detail pages can contain volunteer names, positions, assignments, or training exceptions. Use the dashboard only for authorized Scouting operational purposes. Do not repost personal-detail tables publicly or distribute screenshots more broadly than necessary.

Support

Troubleshooting

A value looks wrong
Clear all filters, confirm the refresh date, and compare the record with the linked workbook.
A district or record is missing
Clear search and page filters. If it remains missing, check whether the source workbook contains the expected row and district label.
The page looks empty
Reload the page. If a filter is active, return it to “Council” or “All.”
The table is cut off
Scroll inside the table horizontally, or print in landscape orientation.
Need assistance
Contact the council dashboard administrator or source-workbook owner. Include the council, page, refresh date, active filters, and a screenshot.
